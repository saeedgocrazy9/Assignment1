THE PROJECT

An Architectural Vision
in Green

Verdant Crest Residences is conceived as a sanctuary of quiet luxury — where low-density towers rise from sculpted landscapes, and every home is designed to breathe. Generous open areas invite nature into daily life, while Italian marble interiors and refined finishes reflect an uncompromising standard.

The architecture draws its geometry from the natural world: clean verticals framed by lush horizontals, sun-drenched terraces, and central courtyards that become the heart of the community. Sustainable design principles ensure this home endures — beautifully.

CURATED LIFESTYLE

A Lifestyle Beyond
the Doorstep

Every amenity is conceived not as a feature but as an invitation to a richer, fuller life.

Grand Clubhouse

A stunning social hub for gatherings, celebrations and leisure.

Multi-purpose banquet halls, lounge areas, card rooms and a fully equipped business centre — all within your community.

Reflexology Garden

Landscaped courtyards with reflexology paths for daily rejuvenation.

Textured stone pathways, aromatic herb borders and shaded seating alcoves create a sensory retreat right at your doorstep.

Swimming Pool

Temperature-controlled lap pool and leisure pool with sun deck.

Resort-style infinity-edge pool with an adjacent shallow splash pool for children, surrounded by Italian stone decking and cabanas.

Wellness Fitness Centre

World-class gym with yoga studio and dedicated wellness rooms.

State-of-the-art cardio and strength equipment, a mirrored yoga studio, steam room and a dedicated aerobics space — open 24 hours.

Children's Play Arena

Safe, vibrant outdoor and indoor play zones for every age.

Toddler-safe soft-play areas, adventure climbing frames, a sand pit and a shaded reading corner — designed with child safety at the forefront.

EV Charging

24/7 Smart Surveillance

AI-powered CCTV, smart access control and 24/7 monitoring.

Party Lawn
